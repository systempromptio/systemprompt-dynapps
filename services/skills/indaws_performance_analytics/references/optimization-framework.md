# Optimization Framework — Performance Analytics

## Process: Measure → Analyze → Hypothesize → Test → Implement

1. **Measure** — Collect data from all channels for the analysis period
2. **Analyze** — Identify patterns and anomalies (flag deviations >20% from trend)
3. **Hypothesize** — Form a testable hypothesis for each anomaly
4. **Test** — Run controlled experiment (A/B or pilot)
5. **Implement** — Roll out winners and iterate

---

## Optimization Levers by Funnel Stage

### Awareness (Top of Funnel)

| Lever | What to optimize | How to test |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| Content topics | Which topics drive most impressions/traffic | Compare topic performance over 4-week periods |
| Content format | Text vs image vs carousel vs video (LinkedIn) | A/B test format with same topic |
| Posting frequency | Posts per week on LinkedIn | Test 3x/week vs 5x/week for 1 month |
| Blog SEO | Target keywords, content depth, internal linking | Track keyword rankings after optimization |
| Event promotion | Invitation channels, messaging, timing | Compare registration rates across channels |

### Consideration (Middle of Funnel)

| Lever | What to optimize | How to test |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| Email nurture sequences | Subject lines, content, send frequency, duration | A/B test subject lines (min 200 recipients per variant) |
| Case studies | Industries covered, depth of results, format | Track which case studies drive most demo requests |
| Website content | Service page messaging, CTA placement, form fields | A/B test CTAs, track conversion rate changes |
| Lead magnets | Downloadable content topics and formats | Compare download rates and lead quality |

### Decision (Bottom of Funnel)

| Lever | What to optimize | How to test |
|-------|-----------------|-------------|
| Demo experience | Demo flow, personalization, follow-up speed | Track conversion rate from demo to opportunity |
| Follow-up speed | Time from form submission to sales contact | Measure impact of faster response times |
| Proposal quality | Template, customization depth, pricing presentation | Track proposal-to-close rate |
| Social proof | Testimonials, case studies shared in sales process | A/B test proposals with/without specific proof points |
| Objection handling | Common objection responses, competitive positioning | Track win rate changes after updating battlecards |

## Prioritization: Impact vs Effort Matrix

Rate each opportunity on two axes (1–5 scale):

```
        High Impact
             |
     Quick   |   Strategic
     Wins    |   Initiatives
             |
Low Effort ──┼── High Effort
             |
     Fill    |   Deprioritize
     Ins     |   (or defer)
             |
        Low Impact
```

**Priority order:**
1. Quick Wins (high impact, low effort) — Do immediately
2. Strategic Initiatives (high impact, high effort) — Plan and schedule
3. Fill-Ins (low impact, low effort) — Do when capacity allows
4. Deprioritize (low impact, high effort) — Defer or eliminate

## A/B Testing Best Practices

**Email subject lines:**
- Test one variable at a time (length, personalization, question vs statement, urgency)
- Minimum sample size: 200 recipients per variant
- Wait 24–48 hours before declaring winner
- Track both open rate AND click rate (open rate alone is insufficient)

**LinkedIn post formats:**
- Test one format change per week
- Minimum 4-week test period per hypothesis
- Compare engagement rate (not just absolute numbers)
- Account for topic differences when comparing formats

**CTA variations:**
- Test button text, color, placement, and surrounding copy
- Minimum 100 visitors per variant before concluding
- Track full conversion (not just click, but form submission)
- Consider page-level and site-level CTAs separately

## Recommendations Format

When presenting optimization recommendations, always include:

```
## Recommendation: [Title]

**Priority:** Quick Win / Strategic Initiative / Fill-In
**Channel:** LinkedIn / Email / SEO / Website / Events / Pipeline
**Hypothesis:** [If we do X, then Y will improve because Z]
**Test approach:** [How to test — A/B, pilot, time-based comparison]
**Success metric:** [Single primary KPI to track]
**Effort:** Low / Medium / High
**Expected impact:** Low / Medium / High
```
