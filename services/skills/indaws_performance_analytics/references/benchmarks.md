## Comparison Periods

| Comparison | Use case | How to interpret |
|-----------|----------|------------------|
| Week-over-week (WoW) | Short-term momentum, campaign impact | Look for spikes/dips caused by specific actions |
| Month-over-month (MoM) | Medium-term trends, channel health | Smooth out weekly noise, identify growth/decline |
| Year-over-year (YoY) | Strategic trends, seasonal comparison | Remove seasonality bias, measure true growth |
| Campaign period vs baseline | Campaign lift measurement | Compare active campaign period to equivalent pre-period |

---

## Trend Identification Process

1. **Collect data** — Pull metrics from all sources for the analysis period
2. **Normalize** — Account for seasonality (compare to same period previous year if available)
3. **Identify anomalies** — Flag metrics that deviate >20% from expected trend
4. **Root cause analysis** — For each anomaly, identify likely cause (campaign, competitor, market, technical)
5. **Trend classification** — Classify as: sustained trend, seasonal pattern, one-time event, or noise
6. **Forecast** — Project next period based on trend and planned activities

## Pipeline-Based Forecasting

**Marketing-sourced pipeline forecast:**
1. Current MQL generation rate (trailing 3-month average)
2. Historical MQL-to-SQL conversion rate
3. Historical SQL-to-Opportunity conversion rate
4. Average opportunity value
5. Historical win rate
6. Expected pipeline = MQLs × Conversion rates × Average value × Win rate
