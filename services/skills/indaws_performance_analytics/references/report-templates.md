# Report Templates — Performance Analytics

## Weekly Marketing Digest (Monday, 1-page summary)

**Purpose:** Quick pulse check for the marketing team.
**Audience:** Victor + marketing team.
**Delivery:** Monday morning, 1 page maximum.

Structure:
- Key Numbers This Week table: Website sessions, LinkedIn impressions, New MQLs, Demo requests, Email campaigns sent (this week / last week / change)
- Highlights: top performing content/campaign, notable win or milestone
- Attention Needed: any metric significantly down, upcoming deadline
- This Week Plan: 3 priorities

---

## Monthly Marketing Report (Detailed)

**Purpose:** Comprehensive performance review with analysis.
**Audience:** Victor + leadership.
**Delivery:** First week of following month.

Structure:
1. Executive summary - 3-5 bullet points of key takeaways
2. KPI dashboard - All channel metrics vs previous month and vs target
3. Channel deep-dives - LinkedIn, Email, SEO, Website, Events (only active channels)
4. Pipeline impact - MQLs, SQLs, Opportunities, Revenue attribution
5. Content performance - Top 5 performing pieces across all channels
6. Competitive intelligence - Notable competitor activity (link to competitive-analysis)
7. Recommendations - Top 3 optimization actions for next month
8. Budget - Spend vs budget, cost per channel

---

## Quarterly Business Review (Strategic)

**Purpose:** Strategic assessment and planning.
**Audience:** Victor + Dynapps leadership.
**Delivery:** End of quarter.

Structure:
1. Quarter in review - Goals vs actuals, major achievements
2. Channel performance trends - 3-month trends with context
3. Pipeline and revenue attribution - Full funnel analysis
4. Market and competitive landscape - Key changes (link to competitive-analysis)
5. Content audit - What worked, what did not, content gaps identified
6. SEO progress - Keyword ranking changes, organic traffic growth
7. Strategic recommendations - Next quarter priorities and goals
8. Budget review and forecast - Spend analysis and next quarter budget request

---

## Campaign-Specific Report

**Purpose:** Evaluate individual campaign performance.
**Delivery:** 1 week after campaign end.

Structure:
1. Campaign overview - Objective, target audience, channels used, duration
2. Results vs goals - Key metrics compared to targets
3. Channel breakdown - Performance by channel (email, LinkedIn, website)
4. Audience insights - Who engaged most (industry, role, company size)
5. Content analysis - Which messages/assets performed best
6. Leads generated - Quality and quantity assessment
7. Lessons learned - What to repeat, what to improve
8. Cost analysis - Total cost, cost per lead, cost per opportunity

---

## Event ROI Report

**Purpose:** Evaluate event investment and return.
**Delivery:** 2 weeks after event.

Structure:
1. Event overview - Type, date, location, objective
2. Attendance metrics - Invitations, registrations, attendees, no-shows
3. Lead capture - New contacts, MQLs generated, quality assessment
4. Pipeline impact - Opportunities created, pipeline value (measure at 30, 60, 90 days)
5. Cost breakdown - Venue, catering, materials, travel, staff time
6. ROI calculation - Cost per lead, cost per opportunity, projected ROI
7. Attendee feedback - NPS or satisfaction scores, qualitative feedback
8. Recommendations - Repeat/modify/cancel decision for future
