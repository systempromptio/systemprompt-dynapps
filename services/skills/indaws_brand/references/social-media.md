## Voice Adjustments

Social media uses the same "Wise Companion" voice with these adaptations:

### Shorter Paragraphs, Same Depth
- LinkedIn/Facebook: 2-4 sentences per paragraph, max 3 paragraphs per post
- Twitter/X: concise but still expert (never superficial)
- Instagram: shorter captions, depth in carousel slides

### Maintained Rules
- ZERO emojis (this applies to social media too)
- Professional "tu" address
- Value vocabulary (inversión, not gasto)
- No "tips rápidos" framing. Present insights as expert analysis

### Tone Differences by Platform
| Platform | Tone Nuance |
|---|---|
| LinkedIn | Most professional. Thought leadership, industry insights |
| Instagram | Slightly more visual-focused copy. Educate through design |
| Twitter/X | Concise expert takes. Link to longer content |
| Facebook | Community-oriented. Client success stories |

## Visual Templates

### Standard Post Layout
- Background: Blue Space or White
- Logo: bottom-left or bottom-right corner, minimum size respected
- Title text: Montserrat Bold, UPPERCASE, Blue Lilac (white bg) or White (dark bg)
- Body text (if any): Montserrat Regular, Blue Space or White
- Accent element: Warm Yellow line or highlight

### Carousel / Multi-slide Post
- Slide 1 (Cover): Blue Space background, title in White, logo centered
- Middle slides: White background, content in Blue Space, headings in Blue Lilac
- Last slide: Blue Lilac or Blue Space background, CTA in Warm Yellow
- Consistent logo placement across all slides (same corner)

### Story/Reel Graphics
- Full-bleed brand color background
- Text: centered, Montserrat Bold, White, UPPERCASE for headline
- CTA sticker area: Warm Yellow background zone at bottom
- Logo: top corner, small (isotipo preferred)

## Content Patterns

### Thought Leadership Post (LinkedIn)
Structure:
1. Opening hook (1 sentence, challenging a common assumption)
2. Expert analysis (2-3 paragraphs explaining the insight)
3. Conclusion with actionable takeaway
4. No CTA "link in bio" style. Let the content speak.

### Client Success (LinkedIn/Facebook)
Structure:
1. Brief context of the client's challenge (use "reto", never "problema")
2. Our approach (what we designed, why)
3. Results (metrics, improvements)
4. Framing: "Te acompañamos en..." not "Les vendimos..."

### Technical Insight (Twitter/X)
Structure:
1. One clear technical statement
2. Brief explanation of why it matters
3. Link to full article/blog if available

### Educational Carousel (Instagram/LinkedIn)
- 5-8 slides
- Each slide: one key concept, minimal text (max 30 words per slide)
- Progressive disclosure: builds from basic to advanced
- Last slide: summary or CTA

## Hashtag & Mention Conventions

### Brand Hashtags
- Primary: `#Indaws`
- Secondary: `#TransformaciónDigital` `#ERP` `#Odoo` (when relevant)
- Industry: use 3-5 relevant industry hashtags per post

### Mention Style
- Tag partners and clients when featuring their success (with permission)
- Tag technology partners (Odoo, etc.) when relevant

### Rules
- Maximum 5-7 hashtags per post (LinkedIn/Instagram)
- Maximum 2-3 hashtags per tweet
- Place hashtags at end of post, never inline
- Never use trending/viral hashtags unrelated to content
