## Report Generation Checklist

Before delivering the report, verify:

### Content Mix
- [ ] 2 TOFU posts included
- [ ] 2 MOFU posts included
- [ ] 1 BOFU post included
- [ ] No 2 consecutive posts of same type
- [ ] No 2 consecutive posts of same funnel phase
- [ ] BOFU is NOT on Monday or Tuesday (needs TOFU/MOFU warm-up)

### Copy Quality
- [ ] Each post has a strong hook (first 2 lines)
- [ ] Zero emojis in all posts
- [ ] Zero hashtags in all posts
- [ ] Value vocabulary used (no "coste", "problema", "gasto")
- [ ] CTA matches funnel phase
- [ ] Links in comments, not in post body
- [ ] Professional "tu" address

### Visual Completeness
- [ ] Each post has a visual brief
- [ ] Dimensions specified
- [ ] Tool/source suggested
- [ ] Carousel slides detailed (if applicable)
- [ ] Real photos specified for team/culture posts (never AI)

### Funnel Strategy
- [ ] Series continuity maintained (if active series)
- [ ] Retargeting opportunity checked from last week
- [ ] Performance analysis included (if data available)
- [ ] Ideas banked for future weeks

---

## Optimal Schedule

| Day | Funnel | Why |
|-----|--------|-----|
| Martes | TOFU | Start week with broad reach, attract new eyes |
| Miercoles | MOFU | Engaged followers from Tuesday see depth |
| Jueves | TOFU | Second reach push, different topic |
| Viernes | MOFU | End week with trust-building content |
| Lunes (next) | BOFU | Warm audience from full week, business mindset on Monday |

Flexibility allowed. The key rule: BOFU never before at least 2 TOFU/MOFU posts that week.
