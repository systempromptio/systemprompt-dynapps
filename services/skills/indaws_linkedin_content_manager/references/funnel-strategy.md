# Funnel Strategy — TOFU / MOFU / BOFU

## The LinkedIn Funnel for Indaws

LinkedIn is not a sales platform. It is a trust-building platform where sales happen as a consequence of consistent value delivery. The funnel structures this process.

```
TOFU (Top of Funnel)     →  "Interesante, no conocia esta empresa"
    ↓
MOFU (Middle of Funnel)  →  "Estos saben de lo que hablan, quiero saber mas"
    ↓
BOFU (Bottom of Funnel)  →  "Necesito hablar con ellos sobre mi caso"
```

---

## TOFU — Attract (2 posts/week)

### Objective
Reach new people who don't know Indaws yet. Maximize visibility.

### Content Principles
- **Generic value**: Content useful to anyone in business, not just Odoo users
- **Low commitment**: No registration, no links to fill — just value
- **Shareable**: Content people want to repost or send to a colleague
- **Broad topics**: Digital transformation, business efficiency, technology trends, team culture

### Post Types
- Opinion / Trend
- Odoo Pill (generic: "how ERPs solve X")
- Behind the Scenes (humanization)
- Educational Carousel (broad topic)

### CTA Patterns
- "Te ha pasado algo parecido?"
- "Que opinas?"
- "Cual es tu experiencia con esto?"
- "Etiqueta a alguien que necesite leer esto"

### Hook Patterns
- Contrarian: "La mayoria de empresas digitalizan mal. Y no es culpa de la tecnologia."
- Question: "Si tu equipo necesita 3 dias para cerrar el mes, el problema no es tu equipo."
- Statistic: "El 67% de las implementaciones ERP fallan en el primer ano. Pero no por la razon que crees."

### Success = Impressions + New Followers
If TOFU posts consistently get <500 impressions, the hooks need work.

---

## MOFU — Educate & Trust (2 posts/week)

### Objective
Convert curious followers into engaged audience. Build trust and authority.

### Content Principles
- **Indaws-specific**: Show how WE do things, our methodology, our results
- **Proof over promises**: Case studies, real numbers, client testimonials
- **Depth**: More detail than TOFU — the reader already cares
- **Expertise**: Technical depth that competitors can't easily replicate

### Post Types
- Case Study
- Podcast / Video Promo
- Carousel (Indaws-specific: "How we implement Odoo in 90 days")
- "How we do it" process posts

### CTA Patterns
- "El caso completo esta en los comentarios"
- "Descubre como lo hicimos — enlace abajo"
- "En el proximo post te cuento como resolvimos [X]"
- "Mira el episodio completo"

### Hook Patterns
- Story: "El director financiero de [Cliente] nos llamo un viernes a las 18:00."
- Result: "De 47 hojas Excel a un dashboard en tiempo real. Esto es lo que hicimos."
- Before/After: "Antes: 3 dias para cerrar mes. Ahora: 4 horas."
- Insider: "Esto es lo que no te cuentan sobre migrar a un ERP nuevo."

### Success = Engagement Rate + Comments
If MOFU posts get lots of impressions but few comments, the content isn't deep enough.

---

## BOFU — Convert (1 post/week)

### Objective
Turn warm audience into leads. Drive direct conversations.

### Content Principles
- **Specific and actionable**: Not "we can help" but "here's exactly what we'd do for you"
- **Results with numbers**: Revenue impact, time saved, ROI metrics
- **Low barrier CTA**: Free diagnostic, 15-minute call, no commitment
- **Urgency without pressure**: Timely (event dates, limited spots) but never pushy
- **Only for warm audience**: BOFU only works if TOFU and MOFU have done their job

### Post Types
- Results / Diagnostic offer
- Event / Demo / Webinar promo
- "Your company has these symptoms" diagnostic post
- ROI calculator or assessment offer

### CTA Patterns
- "Hablamos de tu caso? Te dedicamos 15 minutos sin compromiso."
- "Reserva tu diagnostico gratuito — enlace en comentarios"
- "Quedan [X] plazas para el webinar del [fecha]"
- "Si tu empresa tiene estos 3 sintomas, te interesa leer esto"

### Hook Patterns
- Diagnostic: "Tu empresa tiene estos 3 sintomas. El tercero es el mas peligroso."
- ROI: "Una empresa como la tuya pierde entre 20.000 y 50.000 euros al ano en procesos manuales."
- Event: "El [fecha], vamos a mostrar en directo como [resultado concreto]."
- Testimonial: "'En 6 meses recuperamos la inversion.' — [Nombre], [Cargo], [Empresa]"

### Success = Clicks + Leads + DMs
If BOFU posts get high impressions but zero clicks, the CTA is too vague or the audience isn't warm enough (more MOFU needed).

## Funnel Balance Check

Every Monday, verify:

1. **Are we feeding TOFU?** — Without new audience, the funnel dries up
2. **Are we building MOFU trust?** — Without proof, BOFU falls flat
3. **Is BOFU earning?** — If zero leads from BOFU, either:
   - MOFU isn't warming enough → more case studies
   - BOFU CTA is too aggressive → soften it
   - BOFU content isn't specific enough → add numbers

### Red Flags

| Signal | Diagnosis | Fix |
|--------|-----------|-----|
| High TOFU impressions, low MOFU engagement | Followers don't see us as experts yet | More case studies and depth |
| High MOFU engagement, zero BOFU clicks | Audience trusts us but doesn't need us NOW | Softer BOFU, more diagnostic-style |
| Low everything | Hooks aren't working | Rewrite hooks, test new formats |
| Lots of BOFU clicks, no actual leads | CTA landing experience is broken | Check link destination, simplify form |

## Content Series — Funnel Sequences

The most powerful strategy: linked posts that progressively move audience through the funnel.

### Series Template

**Week N, Day 1 (TOFU):**
"[Broad industry problem or trend]"
→ Educate, generate discussion

**Week N, Day 3 (MOFU):**
"Esto es exactamente lo que paso con [Cliente]. Y como lo resolvimos."
→ Reference the TOFU post, add Indaws proof

**Week N+1, Day 2 (BOFU):**
"Tu empresa tiene los mismos sintomas que [Cliente] tenia. Compruebalo en 2 minutos."
→ Reference both previous posts, offer diagnostic

### Series Examples

**Series: "El reto del inventario"**
1. TOFU: "El 80% de las PYMES gestionan inventario con Excel. Y funciona... hasta que deja de funcionar."
2. MOFU: "Espacio Organico gestionaba 3 tiendas con hojas de calculo. Ahora tiene reabastecimiento automatico."
3. BOFU: "Diagnostico express: responde 5 preguntas y te decimos si tu gestion de inventario tiene riesgo."

**Series: "ERP ≠ Software"**
1. TOFU: "Comprar un ERP no es digitalizarse. Es como comprar un gimnasio y esperar ponerte en forma."
2. MOFU: "Asi es como acompanamos a [Cliente] en su transformacion. Spoiler: el software fue lo de menos."
3. BOFU: "¿Quieres saber que tan preparada esta tu empresa para una transformacion real? 15 minutos, sin compromiso."

Track active series in the weekly report. Max 2 series active simultaneously.

## Retargeting Through Content

When a MOFU post performs exceptionally well (>5% engagement):
1. Note the topic and audience reaction
2. Next week, create a BOFU post on the exact same topic
3. Reference the original post: "La semana pasada hablamos de [tema]. Muchos nos preguntasteis como..."

This mimics paid retargeting but organically — the engaged audience sees the follow-up naturally.
