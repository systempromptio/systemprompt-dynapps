# Content Gap Analysis — Competitive Analysis

## Content Inventory Comparison

For each competitor, inventory their content output:

| Content type | Metrics to track | Evaluation criteria |
|-------------|------------------|---------------------|
| **Blog posts** | Frequency (posts/month), topics covered, average word count, SEO optimization | Consistency, depth, keyword targeting |
| **LinkedIn posts** | Frequency (posts/week), format mix (text/image/video/carousel/document), engagement rate | Engagement quality, follower growth rate |
| **Case studies** | Total published, industries covered, depth of results, format (PDF/web/video) | Specificity of results, credibility |
| **Events hosted** | Frequency, type (webinar/presencial/hybrid), attendance estimates | Reach, lead generation effectiveness |
| **Newsletter / email** | Frequency, signup visibility, content quality (if observable) | Subscriber growth indicators |
| **Video content** | YouTube/LinkedIn video count, production quality, topics | View counts, engagement |
| **Downloadable resources** | Whitepapers, guides, templates, checklists | Lead magnet quality, gating strategy |
| **Odoo App Store modules** | Published modules, downloads, ratings | Technical credibility, community contribution |

## Gap Identification Process

### Step 1 — Map topics covered

List all content topics each competitor has published on. Common Odoo partner topics:
- ERP implementation guides
- Industry-specific use cases (manufacturing, retail, etc.)
- Odoo module deep dives (accounting, inventory, CRM, etc.)
- Digital transformation stories
- Migration guides (from SAP, Dynamics, Sage, etc.)
- Odoo version upgrade guides
- Integration tutorials
- ROI calculators and business case content
- Compliance and legal content (RGPD, SII/TicketBAI, etc.)

### Step 2 — Identify gaps

- Topics covered by 2+ competitors but NOT by Indaws = high-priority gaps
- Topics covered by only 1 competitor = potential differentiation opportunity
- Topics covered by NO competitor = blue ocean content opportunity
- Topics covered by Indaws but NO competitor = unique advantage to amplify

### Step 3 — Quality assessment

Rate each competitor's content on a 1-5 scale:

| Criterion | 1 (Poor) | 3 (Average) | 5 (Excellent) |
|-----------|----------|-------------|----------------|
| **Depth** | Surface-level, generic | Covers basics adequately | Comprehensive, expert-level |
| **Originality** | Copied/rehashed | Standard industry perspective | Unique insights, original data |
| **SEO optimization** | No keyword targeting | Basic optimization | Strong keyword strategy |
| **Visual quality** | Plain text, no images | Stock photos, basic formatting | Custom graphics, professional design |
| **Actionability** | Purely informational | Some practical tips | Step-by-step, immediately useful |
| **Brand consistency** | Inconsistent | Mostly consistent | Strong, recognizable brand voice |

### Step 4 — Prioritize content creation

Use the Content Opportunity Score:

```
Opportunity Score = (Competitor Coverage x 2) + (Search Volume x 3) + (ICP Relevance x 3) - (Our Coverage x 2)
```

Where each factor is rated 1-5. Higher score = higher priority for content creation.

## Battlecard Maintenance

- Review and update battlecards quarterly during deep review cycle
- Flag any battlecard older than 90 days as potentially stale
- Sales team should report competitive encounters for real-time updates
- Track win/loss reasons against each competitor in Odoo CRM
- Never share externally — battlecards are internal sales tools only
- Store in shared location accessible to sales team; format as PDF for offline access

## Competitive Response Playbook

When a competitor makes a significant move:

1. **Assess impact** — Does this affect our positioning, our pipeline, or both?
2. **Document** — Update the relevant battlecard immediately
3. **Communicate** — Alert sales team with a brief summary
4. **Respond** — Determine if a proactive response is needed (content, messaging adjustment, offer)
5. **Monitor** — Track the competitor move's impact over 30/60/90 days
