# Research Methodology — Competitive Analysis

## Primary Sources

| Source | What to extract | Access method |
|--------|----------------|---------------|
| **Odoo Partner Directory** (odoo.com/partners) | Partnership tier, location, team size, verticals, internal rating | Manual review |
| **Competitor websites** | Positioning statements, service pages, pricing models, case studies, blog content | Direct browsing, seo-manager |
| **LinkedIn company pages** | Follower count, posting frequency, engagement rates, team size, hiring activity | Manual export, LinkedIn Analytics |
| **Google My Business** | Review count, average rating, review sentiment, response quality | Google Maps search |
| **Glassdoor / Indeed** | Team size estimates, hiring velocity, company culture signals | Direct search |
| **Odoo Apps Store** | Custom modules published, downloads, ratings | odoo.com/apps filtered by author |

## Secondary Sources

| Source | What to extract | Tool |
|--------|----------------|------|
| SEO visibility and keywords | Domain authority, organic traffic estimates, keyword rankings, backlink profile | seo-manager (DataForSEO API) |
| Social listening | Brand mentions, sentiment, share of voice | Manual monitoring, Google Alerts |
| Industry reports | Market sizing, trends, partner rankings | Odoo official reports, consulting firms |
| Job boards | Open positions indicating growth areas, tech stack clues | LinkedIn Jobs, InfoJobs, Indeed |
| Company registries | Revenue data, employee counts, financial health | CNAE/SABI database, Einforma (public data only) |

## Quarterly Deep Review (January, April, July, October)

1. Update all competitor profiles in `references/competitor-profiles.md`
2. Refresh messaging matrix with current positioning statements
3. Run content gap analysis for the quarter
4. Update battlecards with latest intelligence
5. Reassess positioning map and strategic recommendations

## Monthly Monitoring

1. Check competitor LinkedIn activity (new posts, follower growth)
2. Scan competitor blogs for new content
3. Review competitor website changes (new services, case studies, team members)
4. Track SEO visibility changes via seo-manager
5. Note any new events, webinars, or partnerships announced

## Ad-hoc Triggers

- Competitor launches new service or vertical
- Competitor wins/loses a notable client
- Odoo partnership tier changes
- New entrant to the Spanish Odoo partner market
- Competitor pricing or model changes

## Data Organization

Store all competitor data in structured format within `references/competitor-profiles.md`.
Each competitor gets a dedicated section with standardized fields.
Date-stamp all observations for trend tracking.
