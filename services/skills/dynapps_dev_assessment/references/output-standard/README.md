# Output standard — mandatory reference

These two deliverables **define what the report produced by dev-assessment must look like**.
On any question of form, they rule.

- `01-analisis-rama-master.md` — Security and access, `master` branch
- `02-analisis-rama-staging-visor.md` — same task, `staging-visor` branch

> **Anonymized.** Client, people and task number replaced with placeholders. They are kept as a
> standard of FORM (title, order, how findings are written), not of content.
>
> **They are in Spanish on purpose.** They are real client deliverables, and the output language
> follows the reader (SKILL.md `## Critical`). Imitate their FORM, not their language: for a
> Dutch or French client the same skeleton is written in that language.

They come from a real security-audit engagement. They are **sibling documents and not
interchangeable**: two environments audited separately, each with its own verdict. They also
serve as an example of STEP 0 (two environments = two assessments).

The twelve rules extracted from them are in `../report-and-validation.md`, section "How the
report is written". Read there first; open these only if you need to see a complete example.
