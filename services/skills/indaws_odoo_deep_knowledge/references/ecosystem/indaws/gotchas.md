# Indaws Gotchas — Odoo 18.0

**IG1. indaws_base auto_install triggers cascade installation** — Installing `sale` + `indaws_base` auto-installs `indaws_sale`, `indaws_base_sale`, etc. Impact: installing a new Odoo module (e.g., `mrp`) triggers installation of all matching `indaws_base_mrp` + `indaws_mrp` modules. _ES: Instalé un módulo nuevo de Odoo y de golpe se instalaron solos varios módulos indaws que yo no había pedido._

**IG2. Triple discount is multiplicative, not additive** — `aggregated_discount = 1 - (1-d1)(1-d2)(1-d3)`. Three 10% discounts = 27.1%, NOT 30%. Impact: users expect additive; explain the cascading math. _ES: Apliqué tres descuentos del 10% y el precio no baja un 30% sino un 27%; el cliente no entiende por qué los descuentos no suman._

**IG3. Picking-to-invoice bypasses SO/PO invoice flow** — `indaws_stock_picking_invoice` creates invoices directly from pickings, NOT linked to SO/PO's `invoice_ids` computed field. Impact: reports filtering `sale.order.invoice_ids` may miss picking-generated invoices; check `stock.picking.invoice_ids` too. _ES: Facturé directamente desde el albarán y esa factura no aparece vinculada al pedido de venta ni en su listado de facturas._

**IG4. Rappel settlements generate credit notes automatically** — `rappel.settlement` creates `account.move` (credit note) when validated, appearing in partner's statement. Impact: unexpected credit notes if rappel crons run without review; configure settlement validation as manual. _ES: Aparecen abonos o facturas rectificativas inesperadas en el extracto del cliente sin que nadie las haya creado a mano._

**IG5. indaws_pos_lot_auto_selection auto-selects lots by removal strategy (FIFO/LIFO only — NOT FEFO)** — `indaws_pos_lot_auto_selection/models/pos_order.py:73-89`, `PosOrderLine._get_removal_strategy`: reads `product.categ_id.removal_strategy_id.method`, defaults to `fifo` if unset, then sorts lots accordingly. **FEFO is explicitly NOT supported** (README: "First Expired, First Out is not currently supported"; listed as a roadmap item). Impact: do NOT rely on this module for FEFO/expiry-based lot picking in POS — only FIFO/LIFO are honored. Corrects the prior claim that it resolves POG16/FEFO. _ES: En el TPV los lotes se eligen automáticamente pero no respeta la fecha de caducidad; coge siempre el más antiguo de entrada._

**IG6. indaws_pos_location assigns stock locations to POS order lines (multi-location picking)** — Per its README the module "assigns stock locations to POS order lines for multi-location picking" — i.e. each POS line can be picked from a chosen location, not merely a read-only display popup. NOT VERIFIED at file:line whether selection is purely informational vs. drives the actual stock move; the earlier "visibility-only popup, POS still picks from config warehouse" framing is unconfirmed against source — treat the picking-location effect as real until inspected. _ES: Las líneas del pedido de TPV se sirven desde distintas ubicaciones y no desde el almacén configurado en el punto de venta._

**IG7. Product composition ≠ BOM/Kit — but can coexist** — `indaws_sale_product_composition` is a SALES configurator (child SO lines), not manufacturing. BOM governs stock/MRP, composition governs SO/invoice lines. **They are independent and can coexist on the same product**: a storable with a manufacturing BOM AND composition services creates the physical product via MRP while splitting fiscal lines on the invoice. _ES: Un producto tiene lista de materiales de fabricación y a la vez se descompone en varias líneas en el pedido y la factura._

**IG16. Composition works on ANY product type, not just services** — `indaws_sale_product_composition/models/sale_composition_line_mixin.py:146-154`: `_compute_has_comp` sets `has_comp = bool(product_id.composition_ids) or product_id.composition_configure_new`, NOT `service_tracking`. A storable product with `composition_ids` (computed from `composition_product_ids`) triggers child line creation in SO. `service_tracking='composition'` only feeds `qty_delivered_method` (`indaws_sale_product_composition/models/sale_order_line.py:69-74`, `SaleOrderLine._compute_composition_qty_delivered_method`), it's NOT a prerequisite for compositions to work. _ES: La descomposición en líneas hijas también salta en productos almacenables, no solo en servicios como esperaba._

**IG17. `link_delivered=True` enables cross-type delivery inheritance** — When a composition has `link_delivered=True`, child lines get `qty_delivered_method='parent_composition'` and inherit delivery qty from the parent proportionally. This enables service children to "deliver" when their storable parent ships. Key for mixed-type compositions (storable parent + service children for fiscal breakdown). _ES: Las líneas de servicio se marcan como entregadas al enviar el producto almacenable padre del que cuelgan._

**IG18. Mixed VAT pattern: storable + service compositions** — For products requiring single-item delivery note but multi-tax invoice (e.g., gift baskets with 10% + 21% VAT items): make the main product storable (0€, with manufacturing BOM), add service compositions per tax rate carrying the actual prices with `link_delivered=True`. Result: picking shows only the basket, invoice shows tax-split lines. _ES: En cestas mixtas el albarán muestra solo el producto principal pero la factura lo desglosa en líneas con IVA distinto (10% y 21%)._

**IG19. Parent 0€ line appears on invoice** — The storable parent at 0€ generates its own `account.move.line`. Use `indaws_sale_product_composition_print` with `can_print=False` on the parent to hide it in printed reports, or customize the invoice template to skip zero-amount lines. _ES: Sale una línea a 0€ del producto padre en la factura impresa y quiero ocultarla._

**IG20. `composition_uuid` namespacing avoids cross-document collision** — When propagating to invoices, UUIDs become `{uuid}_sale_order_{order_id}`. If multiple SOs are merged into one invoice, the namespacing prevents UUID conflicts between compositions from different orders. _ES: Al agrupar varios pedidos en una sola factura las líneas de composición se mezclan o entran en conflicto entre pedidos._

**IG21. Recursive compositions nest automatically** — If a child composition product itself has `composition_product_ids`, the system recursively creates sub-children. No depth limit. Impact: accidental circular references can cause infinite loops during `_compute_comp_lines()`. _ES: Odoo se cuelga o entra en bucle al recalcular composiciones por una referencia circular entre productos._

**IG22. `_service_tracking_blacklist` hides compositions from product selectors** — Adding 'composition' to the blacklist means composition-type products won't appear in standard product pickers (POS, expense, etc.). This is for UI filtering, NOT a type constraint. Workaround needed if composition products must be selectable in other contexts. _ES: Los productos de composición no aparecen en el buscador de productos del TPV, gastos u otras pantallas._

**IG8. indaws_data_import uses queue_job (async)** — Integrates with OCA's `queue_job` for async processing; without it, imports run synchronously (blocking). Impact: large imports without `queue_job` block the Odoo process; install it for production. _ES: Las importaciones grandes bloquean o congelan Odoo porque se ejecutan de forma síncrona al faltar queue_job._

**IG9. Sale approval cron only NOTIFIES — it does NOT auto-process** — `indaws_sale_approval`'s cron (`sale_order_approval.py::_cron_send_mails`) emails the salesperson about approvals **already resolved** (`state in ('approved','denied')`); approvals are resolved **manually** via `action_approval` / `action_deny`. Disabling the cron stops the notification emails, NOT the processing — approvals do **not** "freeze". Impact: if approvals seem stuck, it's the manual flow, not the cron. (Corrected after source verification.) _ES: Las aprobaciones de pedido parecen atascadas; en realidad el cron solo envía correos y la aprobación se hace a mano._

**IG10. CashKeeper uses direct socket communication** — `indaws_pos_cashkeeper` communicates via socket (TCP) or middleware mode; network issues = payment failures. Impact: middleware mode provides better resilience than direct socket; ensure stable network. _ES: Fallan los cobros con CashKeeper en el TPV cuando hay problemas o cortes de red._

**IG11. indaws_stock_delivery_portal requires partner portal access** — Delivery portal (signature, DNI, photo) requires customer to have portal access; without it, confirmation URL doesn't work. Impact: ensure `partner.has_portal_access=True` for delivery confirmation customers. _ES: El enlace de confirmación de entrega con firma, DNI o foto no funciona para el cliente._

**IG12. Order type changes sequence and journal** — `indaws_sale_order_type` overrides `SO.name` sequence and invoice journal based on type. Impact: reports grouped by SO number split unexpectedly; filter by `sale_order_type_id` for analysis. _ES: El mismo tipo de pedido usa numeraciones y diarios distintos, y los informes por número de pedido salen partidos._

**IG13. Verifactu sends AFTER posting (does NOT block the post)** — `indaws_l10n_es_edi_verifactu/models/account_move.py:9-20`: `action_post` calls `super()` first, THEN — only when `company.l10n_es_edi_verifactu_required` AND `l10n_es_edi_verifactu_send_auto` AND state is `posted` — builds the Veri*factu document and `_send_as_batch()`. Impact: the invoice is already posted before the send; a Verifactu service/network failure does NOT roll back the post (corrects the prior "may block invoice posting" claim). Auto-send is gated by `l10n_es_edi_verifactu_send_auto`; without it nothing is sent on post. _ES: Un fallo de red o del servicio Veri*factu no impide contabilizar la factura; se contabiliza igualmente._

**IG14. indaws_api uses PBKDF2-hashed opaque tokens (NOT JWT) that expire** — As of `indaws_api` 18.0.1.1.0 the migration moved token storage "from plaintext JWT to PBKDF2-hashed opaque tokens" (`migrations/18.0.1.1.0/pre-migrate.py`). Tokens carry an `expires` timestamp, expired/invalid tokens raise `AccessDenied` 401 (`indaws_api/controllers/token.py:72`, `AccessToken.delete`), and expired tokens are purged via `@api.autovacuum`. Lifetime is `indaws_api.access_token_expires_in` (seconds). Impact: implement token refresh in external integrations; the tokens are opaque (no decodable JWT payload) — corrects the prior "JWT" framing. _ES: Las integraciones externas dejan de conectar por API porque el token caduca y devuelve error 401._

**IG15. Triple discount fields don't appear without the module** — `discount2` and `discount3` fields only exist if `indaws_*_triple_discount` modules are installed. Impact: custom reports referencing these fields fail on databases without the module; check field existence before accessing. _ES: Un informe personalizado falla porque los campos descuento2 y descuento3 no existen en esa base de datos._

## Core-patch gotchas (verified file:line — see [[integrations]] for the full map)

**IG23. Order type RENUMBERS the SO on write** — `indaws_sale_order_type/models/sale_order.py:71-100`, `SaleOrder`: changing `order_type_id` on a draft/sent order re-issues the SO reference from the new type's sequence. Impact: a confirmed-draft order can change its name; reports keyed on SO number break. (Also `create` at :60-69 consumes the TYPE's sequence, not `sale.order`'s.) _ES: Cambié el tipo de un pedido de venta y se le cambió solo el número o la referencia._

**IG24. Invoice journal forced from order type, not standard flow** — `indaws_sale_order_type/models/sale_order.py:102-114`, `SaleOrder._prepare_invoice`: `_compute_journal_id` lets `order_type_id.journal_id` win; `_prepare_invoice` copies it. Impact: invoices ignore the journal you'd expect from the standard sale flow — the order type is source of truth. _ES: La factura del pedido usa un diario distinto al que esperaba del flujo de venta estándar._

**IG25. `sale.order.line.discount` is tier-1, NOT the aggregate (sale≠account)** — sale uses a transient context-manager (`indaws_sale_triple_discount/models/sale_order_line.py:65-118`, `SaleOrderLine._compute_amount`); the stored `discount` is the first discount and the aggregate exists only during `_compute_amount`. Account uses `triple.discount.mixin` where `discount` IS the stored aggregate. Impact: integrations reading `sale.order.line.discount` as "the discount" read tier 1; on the invoice line they read the aggregate. Real migration trap. _ES: El campo descuento de la línea de venta muestra solo el primer descuento y no el total agregado de los tres._

**IG26. Triple discount adds SQL CHECK constraints — legacy data >100% hard-fails** — `indaws_account_triple_discount/models/triple_discount_mixin.py:29-45`, `TripleDiscountMixin._sql_constraints` add DB-level `discount1/2/3 <= 100` CHECKs on `account.move.line`; OCA's `sale_triple_discount/models/sale_order_line.py:75-86`, `SaleOrderLine._sql_constraints` (up to v16) does the same for `discount2`/`discount3` on `sale.order.line`. Impact: importing legacy data with discount >100 fails at the database, not as a soft validation. _ES: Al importar datos antiguos con descuentos mayores del 100% la importación falla directamente en la base de datos._

**IG27. Invoice lines are a stored materialized-path tree** — `indaws_account/models/section_line_mixin.py:8-43`, `SectionLineMixin`: el modelo `section.line.mixin` adds `parent_id`/`parent_path` (`_parent_store=True`) to `account.move.line`. `create` (`:202-211`) / `write` (`:213-218`) call `_compute_parent_id()` when `parent_id` is not in vals and the record has none; `_compute_parent_id` (`:96+`) resolves the parent from `section_parent_uuid` and bails out early if the `no_compute_line_parent_id` context flag is set (`:97`). Impact: raw SQL / bulk writes ignoring `parent_path` corrupt the hierarchy; the rebuild entry point is the public wrapper `rebuild_hierarchy` (`:348`) → `_rebuild_hierarchy_from_source` (`:~287`), which silently `return`s without rebuilding if `len(source_lines) != len(target_lines)` (`:292-293`). _ES: Las secciones o la jerarquía de líneas de factura se corrompen tras escrituras masivas o SQL directo._

**IG28. `cost_summary_ids` deletes+recreates child records on every recompute** — `indaws_sale_margin/models/sale_order.py:12-43`, `SaleOrder.cost_summary_ids`: stored One2many rebuilt via `Command.clear()` + recreate on any cost dependency change (`compute_sudo=True`). Impact: expensive on large orders; child record IDs are NOT stable — never store references to summary lines. `purchase_price_total = amount_untaxed - margin` (`:9-24`), inheriting Odoo `sale_margin` quirks (not a sum of cost lines). _ES: El resumen de costes y márgenes del pedido se recalcula borrando y recreando líneas, y va muy lento en pedidos grandes._

**IG29. Picking↔invoice link is a COMPUTED m2m (no manual relation/join logic)** — `indaws_stock_picking_invoice/models/stock_picking.py:11-40`: `invoice_ids` is a `Many2many` but `compute="_compute_invoice_ids"` + `store=True`, depending on `move_ids.invoice_lines`, mapping `move_ids.invoice_lines.move_id`. The reciprocal `account.move.picking_ids` (`indaws_stock_picking_invoice/models/account_move.py:9-26`, `AccountMove.picking_ids`) maps `invoice_line_ids.invoice_stock_move_id.picking_id` — both sides derive entirely from the custom `account.move.line.invoice_stock_move_id` (`indaws_stock_picking_invoice/models/stock_move.py:23`, `class StockMove` declares its inverse on `stock.move`). Impact: break `invoice_stock_move_id` and the link severs silently. Direction disambiguation in `_get_record_invoice` (`:64-82`): picking carrying both `sale_id` and `purchase_id` (MTO/dropship) → incoming = vendor bill, outgoing = customer invoice (`hasattr` guards avoid hard sale/purchase deps). Move creation is delegated to the sale advance-payment wizard via the bridge `indaws_sale_stock_picking_invoice` (extend `action_invoice_create` / the wizard, not a custom create). _ES: El vínculo entre albarán y factura desaparece solo sin dar ningún error._

**IG30. Rappel volume is invoice-based and counts refunds negatively** — `indaws_rappel/models/rappel.py:430-470`: `_get_period_volume` reads POSTED `account.move.line` filtered by `invoice_date`, subtracting credit-note lines. Impact: draft invoices don't count toward rappel; credit notes reduce the base. `rappel_type` has 7 values (incl. `per_order`, `formula`, `marginal_tiers`), not just fixed/variable (`:30-39`). Settlement creates a CREDIT NOTE (`indaws_rappel/models/rappel_settlement.py:249-326`, `RappelSettlement._create_invoice`), link `rappel_settlement_id` is `copy=False` → duplicating the credit note drops it. _ES: El rappel no cuenta las facturas en borrador y los abonos le restan volumen a la base de cálculo._

**IG31. `indaws_data_export` monkeypatches ORM methods on ARBITRARY models** — `indaws_data_export/models/export.py:487-595`, `IndawsExport._register_hook`: wraps `create`/`write`/`unlink`/`_compute_field_value` on any model chosen as an export target (via `_patch_method`, adapted from `base_automation`). Impact: cross-cutting, interacts with every other override on that model — "why does saving res.partner trigger X?" leads here. Plus `indaws_data_export/models/export_registry.py:14-21`, `convert_to_cache` reassigns `Serialized.convert_to_cache` PROCESS-WIDE at import time. _ES: Al guardar un contacto u otro registro se dispara un comportamiento raro que no sé de dónde sale._

**IG32. The import "god class" is `indaws.import`, not `import_record.py`** — `indaws_data_import/models/indaws_import.py` (~1600 lines). Dispatch is blocking or `queue_job.with_delay` (`:682-706`, hard dep on OCA `queue_job`); `queue.job` `create`/`write` are patched (`indaws_data_import/models/queue_job.py:14-31`, `QueueJob.create`) to auto-advance the import state machine on job `done`. Scheduler manages a backing `ir.cron` (`indaws_data_import/models/indaws_import_scheduler.py:144-259`, `IndawsImportScheduler.create`) — deleting a scheduler deletes its cron. _ES: Al borrar un planificador de importación desaparece también su tarea programada asociada._

**IG33. `dynamic_invoice_section` evals stored Python on the company record** — `indaws_invoice_section/models/res_company.py:6-24`, `DEFAULT_PYTHON_CODE`: `res.company.dynamic_invoice_section` is free-form Python run during invoicing (exposes `env/self/sale/invoice/picking`). This module is `auto_install: False`, depends only on `sale`. Impact: security-review item; arbitrary server-side code per company. _ES: Al facturar se ejecuta código Python personalizado por empresa que provoca comportamientos inesperados._

## v19-specific (see [[v19_changes]] for full detail)

> ⚠️ Scope note (2026-06 verification): this verification pass was against the **v18 source** (`indawsodoo/18/`). The IGV19 entries below assert facts about the v19 codebase and were only spot-checked against `indawsodoo/19/`. Several counts were stale and have been corrected; entries that depend on the live v19 redesign are marked NOT FULLY VERIFIED — confirm against `19/` before relying on them.

**IGV19-1. `_sql_constraints` silently lost in v19** — Odoo 19 no longer applies the legacy list format; `pg_constraint` stays empty, module installs without error. Any migrated indaws model keeping `_sql_constraints` loses its UNIQUE/CHECK silently. Convert to `models.Constraint(...)`. Clean duplicate data BEFORE upgrading or the ALTER fails. (Counts as of 2026-06 in `19/`: ~1 file still on legacy `_sql_constraints`, ~21 files using `models.Constraint` — earlier "0 legacy / 29 Constraint" was stale.) _ES: Tras migrar a v19 desaparecen restricciones de unicidad o validación sin dar error y se cuelan datos duplicados._

**IGV19-2. `Domain` vs `osv.expression` is mixed in 19/** — ~25 files use the new `from odoo.fields import Domain` (`Domain.AND/OR`), ~14 still use `osv.expression`. Don't copy patterns blindly; check the target module first. _ES: En v19 se mezclan estilos de dominios distintos y copiar código de un módulo a otro deja de funcionar._

**IGV19-3. Module set differs between v18 and v19 — verify per module** — As of 2026-06, `18/` has ~154 `indaws_*` modules and `19/` has ~158 (v19 is NOT a smaller subset — the earlier "18=159, 19=~79, ~84 pending" was badly stale). **`indaws_rappel` IS migrated** (`19/indaws_rappel` exists), as are `indaws_dataflow` and `indaws_hr_timesheet`. No `migration_status.yml` was found in `19/` during this pass. Always check the actual `19/` tree before claiming a module is/isn't migrated. _ES: No sé si un módulo indaws concreto está migrado a v19 o no._

**IGV19-4. `indaws_dataflow` is a new base module** — Unifies the import/export ETL engine. `indaws.import.scheduler`/`export.scheduler` now inherit `indaws.dataflow.scheduler` (standalone in v18). Migration gotcha: v18 scheduler **records** aren't migrated automatically → recreate them or lose configured schedulers. Install order: dataflow before import/export. _ES: Tras migrar a v19 se pierden los planificadores de importación y exportación que tenía configurados._

**IGV19-5. Triple discount redesign — `discount` is now the hidden aggregate everywhere** — In v19, sale aligns with account/purchase: `discount1/2/3` are the visible physical fields; `discount` becomes the computed/stored aggregate, hidden in views. Migration moves v18 `discount` → `discount1`. Integrations reading `sale.order.line.discount` as "the discount" now read the aggregate, not the first tier. A create/write shim redirects `{'discount': X}` → `discount1` (loyalty/margin/composition writers). _ES: En v19 el campo descuento de la línea de venta ya no es el primer descuento sino el agregado, oculto en la vista._

**IGV19-6. Rappel `per_order` possibly removed in the v19 redesign — NOT VERIFIED** — Claim: the revamped `indaws_rappel` drops `per_order`/`_compute_per_order`/`target_amount` (rappel_type = fixed/variable/formula only). Status 2026-06: `19/indaws_rappel` EXISTS (contradicting the older "pending, not migrated" framing); the internal `rappel_type` selection of the v19 module was NOT inspected in this pass. In v18 source `rappel_type` has 7 values INCLUDING `per_order` (`indaws_rappel/models/rappel.py:30-41`, `Rappel.rappel_type`). Verify the v19 selection directly before relying on this. _ES: Dudo si el tipo de rappel por pedido sigue existiendo en la versión 19._

**IGV19-7. `indaws_hr_timesheet` is new in v19** — Corporate project-hours/timesheet planning (`project.project.hour(.line)`, `project.project.plan.hour(.line)`, `project.task.hour`). `project.project.hour.line` has no `date_from`/`date_to`, only `date`; hours in HH:MM, not decimal. _ES: En v19 las horas de proyecto usan un único campo de fecha y formato HH:MM, no decimal._
