# OCA Accounting — Gotchas & Friction Points (Odoo 18.0)

Real, source-verified gotchas for the most-used OCA accounting modules. Paths are relative to `OCA/v18/`. Line numbers from v18 branch (2026-06); treat as anchors, re-grep if the file moved.

These modules **patch and extend standard Odoo accounting**. The two loudest install-level facts up front:
- **`account_asset_management` hard-`excludes: ["account_asset"]`** — it CANNOT coexist with Odoo's native asset module. Both occupy the `account.asset` table with incompatible schemas. Installing one requires uninstalling the other.
- **`account_reconcile_oca` replaces the Enterprise reconciliation widget** with an OWL client action + the `account.reconcile.model` `_oca` variant. On a CE database it's the reconciliation UI; on a DB that also has `account_accountant` (Enterprise) the two compete for the same job.

---

## account_reconcile_oca / account_reconcile_model_oca (CE reconciliation widget)

- **OAG1. FX division-by-zero on a 0-amount foreign-currency line** — `account_reconcile_oca/models/account_bank_statement_line.py:1248`, `AccountBankStatementLine._get_exchange_rate_amount`. `_get_exchange_rate_amount` divides by `first(liquidity_lines).amount_currency` with no zero-guard → a foreign-currency statement line with amount 0 raises `ZeroDivisionError` mid-reconcile. Filter/clean zero-amount FX lines before bulk-reconciling. _ES: al conciliar un extracto bancario en moneda extranjera con un apunte de importe cero salta un error y no deja continuar._
- **OAG2. Auto-reconcile can fire on a half-computed structure** — `account_reconcile_oca/models/account_bank_statement_line.py:697-699`, `AccountBankStatementLine._default_reconcile_data`. When a reconcile model returns `auto_reconcile`, `reconcile_bank_line()` runs before suspense-line recomputation fully settles → risk of an unbalanced posting. Review auto-rules before enabling them in bulk. _ES: la conciliación automática cuadra mal y genera un asiento descuadrado al aplicar reglas en lote._
- **OAG3. `_synchronize_to_moves` assumes single liquidity/suspense lines** — `account_reconcile_oca/models/account_bank_statement_line.py:1082-1085`, `AccountBankStatementLine._synchronize_to_moves`. Calls `.id` on what are actually recordsets; multi-line or empty cases yield `AttributeError` or write the wrong partner. _ES: al sincronizar la línea de extracto con los asientos falla o pone el cliente equivocado cuando hay varias líneas._
- **OAG4. Partial-match clamp flips the sign** — `account_reconcile_oca/models/account_reconcile_abstract.py:70-85`, `AccountReconcileAbstract._get_reconcile_line`. Clamping a counterpart to `max_amount` sets `currency_amount = -amount`, producing wrong FX conversion and a mis-sized suspense line on partial reconciliations. _ES: en una conciliación parcial el importe sale con signo cambiado y el pendiente queda mal calculado._
- **OAG5. `unique_matching=True` silently drops multi-candidate matches** — `account_reconcile_model_oca/models/account_reconcile_model.py:525-526`, `AccountReconcileModel._get_invoice_matching_amls_candidates`. With several invoice candidates the model returns nothing instead of surfacing them → the line stays unmatched with NO user-visible reason. Looks like "the rule doesn't work". _ES: la regla de conciliación no propone nada cuando hay varias facturas candidatas y la línea se queda sin conciliar sin avisar._

## account_payment_order / account_payment_mode / account_banking_mandate (bank-payment)

- **OAG6. `draft2open` DELETES and recreates all `account.payment`** — `account_payment_order/models/account_payment_order.py:268`, `AccountPaymentOrder._group_payment_lines`. Re-confirming a draft order wipes any payment data manually entered between draft and open. Don't edit payment records on a draft order expecting them to survive confirmation. _ES: al reconfirmar una orden de pago en borrador se pierden los pagos que había metido a mano._
- **OAG7. `generate_payment_file` is a stub returning `(False, False)`** — `account_payment_order/models/account_payment_order.py:381`, `AccountPaymentOrder.generate_payment_file`. For non-manual methods the SEPA modules MUST override it. If the right module isn't installed you can't tell "no file needed" from "module missing" until `open2generated` fails. _ES: la orden de pago no genera el fichero y no queda claro si falta un módulo o es que no hace falta fichero._
- **OAG8. Uploaded orders can't be deleted, and cancel never unlinks** — `account_payment_order/models/account_payment_order.py:152`, `AccountPaymentOrder.unlink`. Protection forces cancel-before-delete, but cancelled orders then persist forever with no cleanup path. DB accumulates dead payment orders. _ES: las órdenes de pago ya subidas al banco no se pueden borrar y se acumulan órdenes canceladas para siempre._
- **OAG9. Grouped lines collapse to the FIRST line's destination account** — `account_payment_order/models/account_payment_line.py:188-201`, `AccountPaymentLine._prepare_account_payment_vals`. `_prepare_account_payment_vals` sums `amount_currency` across grouped lines but takes `destination_account_id` from the first line only → breaks per-account reconciliation when grouped sources differ. _ES: al agrupar líneas de pago con cuentas distintas todo va a la cuenta de la primera línea y no cuadra la conciliación._
- **OAG10. `post_and_reconcile` reconciles both sides with no balance check** — `account_payment_order/models/account_payment_order.py:428`, `AccountPaymentOrder.post_and_reconcile`. No validation that source/destination lines exist or balance; a prior partial reconcile on the source line leaves a stranded residual. _ES: tras conciliar el pago queda un saldo residual colgado porque la línea ya tenía una conciliación parcial previa._
- **OAG11. Payment-line FX date defaults to `context_today` when unset** — `account_payment_order/models/account_payment_line.py:113`, `AccountPaymentLine._compute_amount_company_currency`. Rate can shift between line creation and order confirmation; a late-set date retroactively revalues grouped lines. _ES: el tipo de cambio del pago cambia entre que creo la línea y confirmo la orden, revalorizando importes._
- **OAG12. Payment-mode journal constraint checks method, not type vs `payment_type`** — `account_payment_mode/models/account_payment_mode.py:78`, `AccountPaymentMode.bank_account_link_constrains`. A mode can point at an incompatible (e.g. outbound-only) journal silently; the mismatch surfaces late at `draft2open`. _ES: el modo de pago deja elegir un diario incompatible y el problema no aparece hasta confirmar la orden._
- **OAG13. Mandate reference unique per company, NOT per bank account** — `account_banking_mandate/models/account_banking_mandate.py:85`, `AccountBankingMandate._sql_constraints`. Multiple valid mandates can exist for the same partner bank account in one company; duplicates aren't prevented. _ES: el sistema permite crear mandatos duplicados con la misma referencia para la misma cuenta bancaria del cliente._

## account_banking_sepa_direct_debit / _credit_transfer / account_banking_pain_base

- **OAG14. Sequence type + `last_debit_date` written only at UPLOAD, not file generation** — `account_banking_sepa_direct_debit/models/account_payment_order.py:295-297`, `AccountPaymentOrder.generated2uploaded`. The FRST→RCUR flip and last-debit stamping happen in `generated2uploaded()`. If upload fails the mandate state is unchanged → a manual retry can resend FRST = **duplicate-FRST risk** the bank rejects. _ES: si falla la subida del fichero SEPA el mandato no actualiza estado y al reintentar se manda un adeudo duplicado que el banco rechaza._
- **OAG15. Changing `partner_bank_id` force-resets sequence to "first"** — `account_banking_sepa_direct_debit/models/account_banking_mandate.py:79-81`, `AccountBankingMandate.mandate_partner_bank_change`. An onchange resets a recurring/final mandate back to FRST when the bank account changes, even if already debited. _ES: al cambiar la cuenta bancaria de un mandato recurrente se resetea a primer adeudo aunque ya se hubiera cobrado antes._
- **OAG16. One-off (OOFF) mandates can never be reused** — `account_banking_sepa_direct_debit/models/account_payment_line.py:44`, `AccountPaymentLine._check_sepa_direct_debit_ready`. Raises `UserError` if `type=="oneoff"` and `last_debit_date` is set → blocks any second payment line on that mandate (by design, but surprises users). _ES: un mandato de pago único no deja hacer un segundo cobro y da error al añadir otra línea._
- **OAG17. Daily cron expires mandates by 36-month inactivity** — `account_banking_sepa_direct_debit/data/mandate_expire_cron.xml`. `_sdd_mandate_set_state_to_expired()` runs daily and can silently invalidate FRST mandates not used within the signature-date window. A long-dormant customer's mandate quietly dies. _ES: un mandato SEPA de un cliente inactivo caduca solo por antigüedad y deja de poder cobrarse sin avisar._
- **OAG18. PAIN version dictates GrpHdr layout** — `account_banking_pain_base/models/account_payment_order.py:327-337`, `AccountPaymentOrder.generate_group_header_block`. `pain.*.001.02` puts `batch_booking`/`Grpg` in the group header; `.03`/`.04` move `batch_booking` to payment-info level and drop `Grpg`. Wrong version → schema-invalid file the bank rejects. The XSD path is built from the version string stored on `account.payment.method` (`account/models/account_payment_method.py:25-35`, `AccountPaymentMethod`). _ES: el fichero SEPA sale con estructura inválida y el banco lo rechaza por usar la versión PAIN equivocada._
- **OAG19. SMNDA amendment indicator emitted for FRST when `last_debit_date` exists** — `account_banking_sepa_direct_debit/models/account_payment_order.py:221`, `AccountPaymentOrder.generate_payment_file`. A previously-debited mandate sent as "first" carries an `AmdmntInd`/SMNDA flag to the bank. _ES: un mandato ya cobrado se envía como primer adeudo y lleva un indicador de modificación que confunde al banco._

## account_asset_management (account-financial-tools)

- **OAG20. HARD CONFLICT — excludes native `account_asset`** — `account_asset_management/__manifest__.py:11`: `excludes: ["account_asset"]`. Both occupy `account.asset` with incompatible schemas. You run one or the other, never both. Migrating Enterprise→CE assets (or vice versa) is a data conversion, not a module swap. _ES: no se puede instalar el módulo de activos OCA junto al de activos nativo, hay que quitar uno para poner el otro._
- **OAG21. Asset-posting cron ships DISABLED, no error notification** — `account_asset_management/data/cron.xml:3-12`, `ir_cron_assets_generator`. `ir_cron_assets_generator` is `active=False` out of the box → depreciation moves are NOT auto-posted until someone enables it; on failure it only logs, never alerts. Classic "why aren't my depreciations posting". _ES: las amortizaciones no se contabilizan solas porque el proceso automático viene desactivado de fábrica y no avisa._
- **OAG22. Final depreciation line forced to `remaining_value`, masking rounding** — `account_asset_management/models/account_asset.py:1079`, `AccountAsset._compute_depreciation_table_lines`. The last line is unconditionally overwritten to the remainder, absorbing all accumulated rounding error silently. _ES: la última línea de amortización se ajusta sola y esconde diferencias de redondeo acumuladas._
- **OAG23. `prorata` auto-forced True for non-yearly `method_time`** — `account_asset_management/models/account_asset.py:358-363`, `AccountAsset._compute_prorrata`. `_compute_prorrata` overrides the profile's prorata setting when `method_time` is `number`/`quarter` → surprises users who set it False. _ES: la amortización activa el prorrateo por su cuenta aunque yo lo hubiera puesto desactivado en el perfil._
- **OAG24. Asset profile swappable until first move posted** — `account_asset_management/models/account_asset.py:396-404`, `AccountAsset._check_profile_change`. You can change the profile between creation and first posting, silently altering the whole depreciation calc with no audit trail. _ES: se puede cambiar el perfil de un activo antes del primer asiento y altera todo el cálculo de amortización sin dejar rastro._
- **OAG25. Posting an invoice with `asset_profile_id` overwrites an existing `asset_id`** — `account_asset_management/models/account_move.py:102-111`, `AccountMove.action_post`. A new asset is created even if the move line already links one → orphans the prior asset without warning. _ES: al validar una factura con perfil de activo se crea un activo nuevo y se queda huérfano el que ya estaba enlazado._

## account_payment_return (account-payment)

- **OAG26. Return move uses only `move_line_ids[0].account_id`** — `account_payment_return/models/payment_return.py:401`, `PaymentReturnLine._prepare_return_move_line_vals`. If a return line bundles payment AMLs from different customer accounts, all use the first one's account → reconciliation mismatches. _ES: la devolución de cobro usa la cuenta del primer apunte y no cuadra cuando hay clientes con cuentas distintas._
- **OAG27. One counterpart reconciled against multiple payments** — `account_payment_return/models/payment_return.py:183-186`, `PaymentReturn.action_confirm`. A multi-payment return line creates a single `return_aml` and reconciles it against all payments; if amounts don't tie out, partial reconciliation breaks silently. _ES: al devolver un recibo que agrupa varios pagos la conciliación se rompe en silencio si los importes no encajan._
- **OAG28. `action_cancel` relies on the return move being posted** — `account_payment_return/models/payment_return.py:233-239`, `PaymentReturn.action_cancel`. It reads `matched_credit_ids` of the return move; if `_post()` had failed that's empty and the invoices are never re-reconciled (stay "paid" though the payment bounced). _ES: al cancelar una devolución las facturas siguen marcadas como pagadas aunque el cobro haya sido devuelto._
- **OAG29. Bank-return fees miscategorized** — `account_payment_return/models/payment_return.py:416`, `PaymentReturnLine._prepare_expense_lines_vals`. Expense/charge line uses `journal.default_account_id`, not `default_expense_account_id` → the clearing pair reverses against the bank account instead of the charge account. _ES: los gastos por devolución bancaria van contra la cuenta del banco en vez de contra la cuenta de gastos._

## account_financial_report (account-financial-reporting)

- **OAG30. `target_move` defaults to "posted" — draft entries silently excluded** — `account_financial_report/wizard/general_ledger_wizard.py:29-33`, `GeneralLedgerReportWizard.target_move`. Users reconciling against draft moves see gaps without realizing it. Set "all entries" when auditing drafts. _ES: el informe financiero no muestra los asientos en borrador porque por defecto solo saca los contabilizados._
- **OAG31. FX initial balance distorted on multi-currency accounts** — `account_financial_report/report/general_ledger.py:872-888`, `GeneralLedgerReport._get_report_values`. A conditional `bal_curr` subtraction zeroes the FX initial balance, distorting the end balance for currency-differing accounts. _ES: en cuentas multidivisa el saldo inicial en moneda extranjera sale distorsionado y el saldo final queda mal._
- **OAG32. Trial balance groups by currency but merges by account** — `account_financial_report/report/trial_balance.py:454-491`, `TrialBalanceReport._get_data`. `read_group` includes `currency_id` in groupby yet results merge on `account_id` only → multi-currency accounts can double-count or lose `amount_currency`. _ES: el balance de sumas y saldos duplica o pierde importes en cuentas con varias divisas._
- **OAG33. Journal ledger reads tax relations via raw SQL** — `account_financial_report/report/journal_ledger.py:213`, `JournalLedgerReport._get_move_lines`. `cr.execute` on `move_line_account_tax_rel` bypasses ORM/record rules → skips security and risks stale tax data. _ES: el diario del informe puede mostrar datos de impuestos desactualizados y saltarse permisos de acceso._
- **OAG34. Trial balance does an O(n) in-memory rebuild to sort partners** — `account_financial_report/report/trial_balance.py:376-391`, `TrialBalanceReport._compute_partner_amount`. Real CPU/memory hit on accounts with many partners. Budget time on large datasets. _ES: el balance de comprobación va muy lento y consume mucha memoria en cuentas con muchos clientes._

## account_statement_import_camt / _file / _base (bank-statement-import)

- **OAG35. Multi-statement CAMT file keeps only the LAST statement's currency/IBAN** — `account_statement_import_camt/models/account_statement_import_camt_parser.py:459-465`, `AccountStatementImportCamtParser.parse`. Looping `Stmt` nodes overwrites currency and `account_number` → a file mixing accounts gets the wrong currency/IBAN match. _ES: al importar un CAMT con varios extractos se queda solo con la divisa y el IBAN del último y casa la cuenta equivocada._
- **OAG36. ZIP import concatenates all statements into one** — `account_statement_import_camt/models/account_statement_import.py:28-31`, `AccountStatementImport._parse_file`. Recursive `_parse_file` merges every XML's transactions into a single list with only the last currency/account → flattens multi-account archives. _ES: al importar un ZIP de extractos bancarios los junta todos en uno solo con una única cuenta y divisa._
- **OAG37. Currency mismatch checked only AFTER IBAN journal match** — `account_statement_import_file/wizard/account_statement_import.py:209-296`, `AccountStatementImport._match_journal` (check at :286). A wrong-currency file may already have created bad journal associations before the error fires. _ES: el fichero de extracto con divisa incorrecta crea asociaciones de diario erróneas antes de dar el error._
- **OAG38. Dropped duplicate's amount is added back to `balance_start`** — `account_statement_import_file/wizard/account_statement_import.py:337-338`, `AccountStatementImport._create_bank_statements`. When a duplicate (matched by `unique_import_id`) is dropped, its amount shifts `balance_start` → desyncs computed balance from `balance_end_real`. _ES: al descartar un movimiento duplicado el saldo calculado del extracto deja de cuadrar con el saldo real._
- **OAG39. Missing `account_number` weakens `unique_import_id`** — `account_statement_import_base/models/account_journal.py:56-65`, `AccountJournal._statement_line_import_update_unique_import_id`. Without an account number the dedup ID drops its account prefix → collisions likely across files even while IBAN matching still succeeds. _ES: sin número de cuenta en el extracto se detectan mal los duplicados y se cuelan movimientos repetidos entre ficheros._
- **OAG40. Zero-amount CAMT detail splits silently ignored** — `account_statement_import_camt/models/account_statement_import_camt_parser.py:185-187`, `AccountStatementImportCamtParser.parse_transaction_details`. `TxDtls` amounts update the transaction dict only when non-zero → legitimate zero splits vanish. _ES: al importar CAMT desaparecen sin avisar los desgloses de movimiento con importe cero._

## account_cutoff_base / account_cutoff_picking (account-closing)

- **OAG41. Cutoff price taken from the LAST invoiced line, not weighted average** — `account_cutoff_picking/models/account_cutoff.py:322`, `AccountCutoff.order_line_update_oline_dict_from_invoice_lines`. Several pre-cutoff invoices at different prices → only the last invoice's `price_unit` is used, mispricing the accrual. _ES: la periodificación de cierre toma el precio de la última factura en vez del promedio y valora mal el ajuste._
- **OAG42. Cutoff date filter is timezone-sensitive** — `account_cutoff_picking/models/account_cutoff.py:298`, `AccountCutoff.order_line_update_oline_dict_from_invoice_lines`. `invoice.date <= cutoff_date` (date-only) for near-midnight postings can include/exclude an invoice depending on user TZ vs UTC. _ES: facturas de fin de mes cerca de medianoche entran o quedan fuera del cierre según la zona horaria del usuario._
- **OAG43. `back2draft` + regenerate can duplicate lines** — `account_cutoff_base/models/account_cutoff.py:120-125`, `AccountCutoff.company_currency_id`. Unique constraint only on `(date, company, cutoff_type)`; `get_lines()` unlinks/recreates, so concurrent/repeated state toggling before the move is deleted duplicates lines. _ES: al volver a borrador y regenerar el cierre se duplican líneas._
- **OAG44. Per-line rounding then summed can imbalance the entry** — `account_cutoff_base/models/account_cutoff.py:231`, `AccountCutoff.back2draft`. Each merged amount is rounded individually while the counterpart recomputes from a fresh sum → high-decimal currencies can produce a 0.01 imbalance. _ES: el asiento de cierre queda descuadrado por céntimos en monedas de muchos decimales por redondeos línea a línea._
- **OAG45. `analytic_distribution` merge keys on `json.dumps()` string** — `account_cutoff_base/models/account_cutoff.py:237-240`, `AccountCutoff._get_merge_keys`. Two equivalent distributions that serialize with different key order won't merge → splits what should be one cutoff line. _ES: la distribución analítica no se fusiona y se parte en varias líneas de cierre aunque sea la misma._

## account_analytic_required (account-analytic)

- **OAG46. "posted" policy is a hard block AT posting, not before** — `account_analytic_required/models/account.py:82-85`, `AccountMoveLine._check_analytic_required_msg` + check runs after `super()._post()` (:44). Draft lines without analytic are allowed; the error appears only at posting time and rolls back an already-completed post rather than blocking up front. _ES: la línea sin analítica se deja guardar en borrador pero da error justo al contabilizar y deshace el asiento._
- **OAG47. "always" policy blocks on every create/write** — `account_analytic_required/models/account.py:57`, `AccountMoveLine._check_analytic_required_msg`. The `@api.constrains` fires on any `analytic_distribution`/account/debit/credit change → prevents even saving a draft line without analytic. _ES: no deja ni guardar un borrador sin cuenta analítica porque el control salta en cada cambio._
- **OAG48. Only fully-zero (debit AND credit 0) lines bypass the check** — `account_analytic_required/models/account.py:54-55`, `AccountMoveLine._check_analytic_required_msg`. A zero-debit / non-zero-credit line still requires analytic; there's no account-type exemption. _ES: una línea con importe solo en el haber sigue exigiendo cuenta analítica; solo se libra si debe y haber están a cero._
