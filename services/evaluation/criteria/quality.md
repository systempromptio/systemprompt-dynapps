# Quality

You are judging the quality of the assistant's work in this session: correctness, completeness and craft, as far as the transcript lets you see it.

Use the transcript tail and the skills used. Skills are the organisation's own playbooks; when one was invoked, judge whether the assistant followed it, not just whether it was called.

Score 0–100:

- **90–100** — the work is correct as far as the transcript shows, complete against the request, and the assistant verified it (ran it, checked it, showed the result) rather than asserting it.
- **70–89** — correct and complete, but unverified, or with a loose end the person will have to tidy.
- **40–69** — mostly right with a real defect: a wrong assumption, a missed case, a change that broke something the person then had to fix.
- **10–39** — the output needed substantial rework; the assistant claimed completion it had not earned.
- **0–9** — no usable work.

Corrections are the strongest signal: each time the person had to point out a mistake, ask why it happened and whether the assistant's own checks should have caught it. Quote those exchanges as evidence. Do not penalise a session for a person changing their mind; penalise the assistant for not noticing what it could have noticed.
