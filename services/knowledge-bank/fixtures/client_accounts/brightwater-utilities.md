# Client brief - Brightwater Utilities

## Company and industry

Brightwater Utilities is a regulated water and energy supplier in the south west of England serving residential and business customers. The engagement, completed in March 2026, replaced their customer self-service portal with a Salesforce Core Experience Cloud site for account management, meter readings, billing and moving home, integrated with their billing system and their field service scheduling. The account is now a reference client and a source of case-study material; there is no active work.

## Platforms

Salesforce Core Service Cloud and Experience Cloud, Field Service for engineer scheduling, a legacy billing system integrated through a middleware layer, and an identity provider shared with their mobile application. No commerce platform: the account is relevant to the storefront programme only as a reference for Salesforce Core delivery quality and for the runbook discipline that was first written there and later adapted for Northwind.

## Engagement scope

The engagement was a fourteen-month fixed-price build with a three-month hypercare period that ended in June 2026. Deliverables were the self-service site, the billing and field service integrations, the moving-home journey, and the operational runbooks that Tom's team now runs. A closing review recorded the engagement as Green with lessons on integration testing that were carried into the Northwind programme's runbooks.
