# Client brief - Halden & Voss

## Company and industry

Halden & Voss is an Oslo-based premium furniture and interiors brand with eleven showrooms in Norway, Sweden and Denmark and a storefront that accounts for a third of revenue. The showroom experience is the brand: customers configure sofas and storage in store with a consultant and complete the purchase online later, or the reverse. The company is privately held, conservative in its technology choices, and values stability over features. The engagement is a support and small-change retainer following a storefront build completed in 2025.

## Platforms

Salesforce Commerce Cloud with a Storefront Next front end, an in-house product configurator that predates the storefront and is exposed through an API, a showroom appointment system on Salesforce Core, and a European 3PL for delivery and assembly scheduling. The configurator is the single most valuable and most fragile piece of the estate and the reason the PDP Configurator work on Northwind is of interest to them: a platform-native configurator would let them retire their own.

## Key contacts

Ingrid Halden, Chief Executive, sponsor and family owner, engaged at the quarterly review only. Erik Voss-Lund, Digital Director, the day-to-day decision maker and the person who approves every change on the retainer. Kari Strand, Showroom Operations Lead, the voice of the consultants and the person who will judge any configurator change. Astound Digital's engagement manager is Miriam Okafor, with Tomasz Wieczorek as BA on the retainer and one developer allocated at half time.

## Engagement scope

A twelve-month retainer of forty days per quarter covering support, platform upgrades of the reference application, small changes approved by Erik, and a quarterly review. Current retainer work: the summer reference application upgrade, a change to the showroom appointment booking to show consultant availability by product category, and an assessment of replacing the in-house configurator with a platform-native one based on the Northwind PDP Configurator pattern.

## Open opportunities

The configurator replacement, which would be a fixed-price project of six to nine months if the assessment recommends it and Kari's consultants accept the prototype. A second-hand and showroom-sample sales channel that Erik has mentioned twice. A Marketing Cloud implementation to replace their newsletter tool, which sales operations own and Astound has not yet been asked about.

## Risks

The retainer is consumed faster than planned by upgrade work, which threatens the configurator assessment. The in-house configurator API has no documentation and the one engineer who knows it is leaving in October. Erik's approval cadence is slow and a change that misses a quarterly review waits a quarter.
