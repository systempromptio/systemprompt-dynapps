# Client brief - Larkspur Outdoors

## Company and industry

Larkspur Outdoors is a Bristol-based direct-to-consumer brand of technical outdoor apparel and camping equipment, selling in the United Kingdom and the Nordics with a strong repair-and-resale programme that is central to its brand. Growth has come from community content and from a subscription for consumables such as waterproofing and repair kits. The business is venture backed, moves fast, and is unusually willing to run experiments in production if they are instrumented. Their margin structure makes a saved-payment subscription flow and a frictionless repeat purchase the commercial priorities.

## Platforms

Larkspur runs Salesforce Commerce Cloud with a Storefront Next front end launched in spring 2026, an in-house subscription service, a returns and repair portal built on Salesforce Core Service Cloud, and a PSP that supports network tokens. Their engineering team is small and capable and prefers to own the code after handover, so the engagement is structured as an enablement pilot rather than a managed build.

## Key contacts

Ravi Menon, Head of Engineering, technical counterpart and the person who will own the code after the pilot. Freya Lund, E-commerce Manager, product owner for the pilot scope and the source of the analytics targets. Astound Digital's engagement manager is Miriam Okafor with Ana Lucia Ferreira as architect and Ben Carver on loan for the pilot's storefront work.

## Engagement scope

A six-week fixed-price pilot to bring the Express Checkout pattern proven on Northwind Trading to Larkspur's storefront, adapted to their subscription flow, with a knowledge handover to Ravi's team. The pilot delivers the eligibility service, the express panel, the analytics events and a handover workshop, and it is measured on repeat-purchase conversion for the pilot cohort. The Larkspur pilot ships on the third Tuesday of September, which Oona chose to land before their autumn campaign, and the date is fixed while the scope is flexible.

## Open opportunities

A full storefront programme on the Northwind model if the pilot hits its conversion target, which Freya has said she would sponsor. A Service Cloud integration for the repair portal so that a repair order shows on the customer's storefront account. A resale marketplace feature that the founders have talked about publicly and that no partner has scoped.

## Risks

The fixed date with a flexible scope needs disciplined scoping at every weekly check-in, and Freya's analytics targets are ambitious for a six-week window. Ben Carver's loan reduces Northwind capacity during the same weeks that EAICOMM-107 must close. Ravi's team has never run the reference application's upgrade, so the handover workshop must cover extend-not-fork explicitly or the pilot code will drift.
