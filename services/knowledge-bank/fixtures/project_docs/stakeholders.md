# Stakeholders - Northwind Trading storefront programme

## Client side

Northwind Trading's executive sponsor is Helen Marsh, Chief Digital Officer, who owns the storefront budget and chairs the monthly steering committee; she cares about conversion, launch dates, and being told early when something slips. The product owner is Daniel Reyes, Head of E-commerce, who attends every sprint review, approves stories for release, and is the tie-breaker on scope. Sunita Bhatt, Head of Customer Service, represents the contact centre and must sign off on any change to order editing, returns, or saved payment behaviour. Marcus Feld is Northwind's IT lead and owns the ERP and OMS integrations, their change calendar, and the production access approvals; nothing reaches production without his change ticket.

## Astound Digital side

Miriam Okafor is the engagement manager and the single point of escalation for Northwind. Priya Raman is the lead business analyst and owns the functional specifications, the writing standards, and the backlog grooming with Daniel. Tomasz Wieczorek is the second BA, currently owning the PDP Configurator specification. Ana Lucia Ferreira is the technical architect and signs off every solution design, cartridge selection and integration pattern. Ben Carver leads the storefront development team of four, Kofi Mensah leads the two integration developers, and Lena Sørensen is the QA lead who owns the test plans, the regression pack and the release sign-off checklist. Jonas Kraft from the Salesforce Core team supports the CRM objects that back the engagement reporting.

## Decision rights

Scope changes within a sprint are agreed between Priya and Daniel and recorded in Jira; scope changes affecting the release plan go to the steering committee. Solution design decisions are Ana Lucia's; where they change cost or date they are raised to Miriam and Helen. Release go/no-go is a joint call by Lena, Ben, Kofi, Daniel and Marcus at the release readiness meeting, with Miriam holding the record. Production incidents are declared by whoever detects them and run according to "Runbook - SFCC storefront incident"; Marcus is the client-side incident contact around the clock during a release week and during trading peaks.

## Meeting cadence

Daily stand-up at 09:30 CET with the delivery team; a client-facing sprint review every second Thursday at 14:00 CET; backlog refinement every Tuesday at 11:00 CET; the steering committee on the first Wednesday of the month; and a release readiness meeting the Monday before every production release. Minutes for client-facing meetings are written by the BA on duty within a day and stored in the Confluence space EAICOMM under "Meeting notes", and any decision made in a meeting is also recorded on the relevant Jira ticket so that the ticket, not the minutes, is the source of truth.

## Communication rules

Client-facing channels are the shared Slack channel and the weekly status email from Miriam; developers do not email Northwind staff directly except within an incident. Every Jira ticket that a Northwind stakeholder will read follows "BA writing standards", and every specification page carries a status and an owner. Risks are logged in the RAID log on the programme page and reviewed at the steering committee; a risk that becomes an issue is raised to Miriam the same day.
